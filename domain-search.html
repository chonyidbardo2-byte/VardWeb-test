Domain Broker Service

Here's how this works:

  1. You get a dedicated domain broker.

    When you purchase the service, a dedicated broker will help you formulate a strategy and budget to try to acquire your domain.

  2. The domain broker works the deal for you.

    They go to work with the domain owner, trying to get you the lowest possible price. (Don't worry, your identity remains confidential.)

  3. The domain is securely transferred to your account.

    If the owner agrees to the sale, you'll pay the negotiated sale price plus a 15% commission. Then the domain is securely transferred to you. VardWeb handles all steps of the transaction, giving you peace of mind.

Domain Questions, Answered

A domain is the human-readable address people type to find your site, instead of the long string of numbers a server actually uses. DNS quietly translates one into the other every time someone visits. Owning your own domain (rather than a subdomain or a social profile) is what makes a business look established, and it's the foundation everything else — your website, your hosting, your brand — gets built on.
Type it into the search bar at the top of this page — we'll tell you instantly if it's open, taken, or available on a different ending.
