Hosting
Agreement

What's covered when VardWeb hosts your site, and what stays on you.

Last updated: September 2026

// 01 — scope of this agreement

What “hosted by VardWeb” actually means.

This agreement applies when VardWeb provides hosting for a site we've built or maintain for you — bundled into a site-build package (Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Custom) or arranged separately. If you host elsewhere and only use VardWeb for domain registration, this agreement doesn't apply to you; see the Domain Name Proxy Agreement instead.

// 02 — infrastructure & uptime

We don't control the infrastructure. Here's what that means.

Hosting runs on Cloudflare's network and other third-party infrastructure providers. We use commercially reasonable efforts to keep sites available and performant, but we don't control, and can't guarantee against, outages, latency, or disruptions originating at that infrastructure layer, upstream internet providers, or DNS resolution outside our systems.

// 03 — acceptable use

What can't live on our servers.

Hosted content can't include anything illegal, malware or phishing infrastructure, spam origination, content that infringes someone else's rights, or anything that meaningfully abuses shared server resources at the expense of other customers. We can suspend hosting immediately, without notice, for a clear violation.

// 04 — backups & your data

Back it up like it's yours. It is.

We take reasonable measures to protect hosted data, but you're responsible for keeping your own independent copies of anything you can't afford to lose — content, media, code, and configuration. VardWeb isn't liable for data loss beyond what's covered under our Terms of Service's limitation of liability.

// 05 — suspension & termination

How hosting can end.

We may suspend or terminate hosting for non-payment, a violation of acceptable use, or at the end of a service agreement. You can request your files and any exportable data before an account closes — we don't hold your content hostage over a dispute.

// 06 — domains & hosting are separate

One invoice doesn't mean one contract.

Hosting a site and registering the domain it lives on are governed separately — this agreement covers hosting; the Domain Name Proxy Agreement covers the domain's WHOIS privacy. Bundling them into one package for convenience doesn't merge their terms.

// 07 — limitation of liability

The same caps as our Terms of Service.

VardWeb's liability arising from hosting services is limited exactly as set out in our Terms of Service's limitation-of-liability section — this agreement doesn't expand or duplicate that cap, it operates under it.

// 08 — governing law

Canadian law. Same as everything else.

This agreement is governed by the laws of Canada and the province in which VardWeb operates, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

// 09 — questions & contact

Questions about your hosting?

If you have any questions about what's covered, want to discuss an outage, or need to raise a concern about an active hosting account, reach out directly. We respond promptly and without friction.
