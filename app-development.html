Why an App Costs Monthly

Apps need looking after even when nothing changes. Apple and Google move the goalposts every year, and your monthly fee keeps you on the right side of them.

Android's Yearly Deadline

Every August, Google raises the minimum Android version apps must target. We update yours before the deadline, so it never stops receiving updates.

Every New iOS

Store Paperwork

Crash Monitoring

Hosting and Database

From Idea to Store

Six steps from kickoff to launch. App timelines start once your Apple and Google developer accounts are approved, so we start that paperwork on day one.

  1. Kickoff and accounts

    We set up your Apple and Google developer accounts and request your business number. Build time starts once they're approved, which can take up to 30 days.

  2. Design

    Screens designed around the one thing your customers come back for, in your brand.

  3. Build

    We build your app on the same code as your site, with accounts, push and offline mode from day one.

  4. Test

    You try it on your own phone through Apple's TestFlight and Google's closed testing before anyone else sees it.

  5. Store review

    Apple and Google review every app. If they ask for changes, we make them and resubmit at no charge.

  6. Launch and grow

    Your app goes live in the stores, analytics land in your dashboard, and updates keep coming every month.

A PWA installs straight from the browser, needs no store review and updates instantly. Store apps are easier to find, can use more phone features and carry the trust of an Apple or Google listing. Many clients start with a PWA and add the stores later, on the same code.
From about 4 to 6 weeks for Bronze to 3 to 4 months for Gold. App timelines start once your Apple and Google developer accounts are approved. Business verification can take up to 30 days, so we start it on day one.
