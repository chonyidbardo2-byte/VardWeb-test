// Monthly

Why an app costs monthly.

Apps need looking after even when nothing changes. Apple and Google move the goalposts every year, and your monthly fee keeps you on the right side of them.

Android's yearly deadline

Every August, Google raises the minimum Android version apps must target. We update yours before the deadline, so it never stops receiving updates.

Every new iOS

Apple releases a new iOS each September and new tooling each spring. We test, fix and ship before your customers notice.

Store paperwork

Privacy labels, Data safety forms and age ratings change often. We keep them current, so your listing never gets pulled.

Crash monitoring

We see crashes before the reviews do, and fix them with an instant update.

Hosting and database

Your app's backend, database and daily backups, hosted and managed for you.

Small changes

New text, images and tweaks, shipped to every platform at once.

// How It Works

From idea to store.

  1. 01

    Kickoff & accounts

    We set up your Apple and Google developer accounts and request your business number. Build time starts once they're approved, which can take up to 30 days.

  2. 02

    Design

    Screens designed around the one thing your customers come back for, in your brand.

  3. 03

    Build

    We build your app on the same code as your site, with accounts, push and offline mode from day one.

  4. 04

    Test

    You try it on your own phone through Apple's TestFlight and Google's closed testing before anyone else sees it.

  5. 05

    Store review

    Apple and Google review every app. If they ask for changes, we make them and resubmit at no charge.

  6. 06

    Launch & grow

    Your app goes live in the stores, analytics land in your dashboard, and updates keep coming every month.

// frequently asked

App Questions, Answered

Apple only accepts apps that offer more than a repackaged website, so every build includes real app features like push notifications, offline mode and Face ID sign-in. If a review comes back with changes, we make them and resubmit at no charge. If Apple still says no after three rounds, we refund the App Store portion of your setup fee.
Not on products or services. Anything your customers buy and use in the real world, like orders, bookings and deposits, is paid through Stripe, and the stores take nothing. Only digital goods, such as paid in-app content, must use the store's own billing, which charges about 15%.
A PWA installs straight from the browser, needs no store review and updates instantly. Store apps are easier to find, can use more phone features and carry the trust of an Apple or Google listing. Many clients start with a PWA and add the stores later, on the same code.
From about 5 to 7 weeks for Bronze to 4 to 6 months for Gold. App timelines start once your Apple and Google developer accounts are approved. Business verification can take up to 30 days, so we start it on day one.
Your store listings, reviews, app name and all of your data are yours. VardWeb owns and maintains the code, which your monthly fee covers. If you ever leave, we transfer your data, domain and analytics into accounts you own, and you replace or unpublish the app within 30 days.
Apple charges about C$140 a year and Google about C$35 once. We pay them so your membership never lapses, which would pull your app from the store, then bill you at cost with no markup.
No Mac needed, we build in the cloud. Your app does need Apple and Google developer accounts in your business's legal name, and we walk you through both. If you're a sole proprietor, the stores show your personal legal name as the seller, and Google adds a two-week testing period before launch.
