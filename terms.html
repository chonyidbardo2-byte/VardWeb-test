// 02 — services we provide

What VardWeb builds and maintains.

VardWeb offers custom web design, development, and digital services. The specific scope of work for any engagement is defined in a written proposal or statement of work agreed upon by both parties. Our services may include, but are not limited to:

  • Custom website design & development: responsive, bespoke sites built to your brand and business requirements.
  • Content Management System (CMS) implementation: allowing you to update your own site content without developer involvement.
  • Domain & hosting facilitation: assistance registering, configuring, and managing domains and third-party hosting providers on your behalf.
  • Ongoing maintenance & support plans: recurring service agreements covering updates, security monitoring, and technical support as specified in your plan.
  • SEO reporting & recommendations: performance audits, accessibility reviews, and actionable reports. We do not guarantee specific search engine rankings.
  • Client portal & CRM access: a secure portal through which you can view project details, invoices, and manage your site content.

Team Seats

Dashboard seats are for your own staff. Each seat is one person with their own login. Plans with unlimited seats are subject to fair use.

Variants & Testing

Landing page plans include a set number of new page variants each month. Unused variants do not roll over to the next month. Live A/B tests run when the page receives enough traffic to give a reliable result; otherwise variants are compared month over month and reported as directional, not proven.

Landing Page Edits

The self-editing CMS described above does not apply to landing pages. Landing pages are edited by VardWeb only, so every change can be tested. You can view your page and its results in your dashboard at any time.

Optional Features

Online payments, booking and discount codes are set up on request at no extra charge from VardWeb. You pay the provider's own transaction fees directly, for example Stripe's card processing fees.

App Store Accounts

Apps are published under your business's own Apple and Google developer accounts, because both stores require an app to be listed by the business it represents. You enroll in your legal name, and VardWeb is added as Admin to build, submit and maintain your app. VardWeb pays the store membership fees and re-bills them at cost: Apple's yearly fee as a yearly invoice line, and Google's one-time fee on your setup invoice. While your service is active, please don't remove VardWeb's access or cancel the memberships, as either can take your app out of the store.

Store Review

Apple and Google review every app and every update. If a review is rejected, VardWeb makes the changes and resubmits at no extra charge, but we can't guarantee approval. If Apple still rejects your app after three rounds of changes, VardWeb refunds the App Store portion of your setup fee, and your Android and web app builds continue as part of your service.

Payments Inside Apps

Products and real-world services sold in your app are paid through Stripe, and Apple and Google take no commission on them. Digital goods and content must use the store's own in-app purchase system, and the store's commission on those sales is your cost.

VardWeb reserves the right to decline, modify, or discontinue any service offering at any time. Changes to an agreed scope of work require written consent from both parties and may be subject to additional fees.
