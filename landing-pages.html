// frequently asked

Landing Questions, Answered

You own your domain and everything the page generates: your content, every lead and every customer record, exportable whenever you ask. VardWeb owns and maintains the underlying code and the hosting accounts it runs on, which is what your monthly rate covers. If you leave, we move your data, database, domain settings and email-sending setup into accounts you own within 30 days of your final payment.
A Bronze page typically launches in about 1 week, Silver in 1 to 2 weeks, and Gold’s two pages in 2 to 3 weeks. Custom programs are scheduled at discovery. We’ll confirm a firm timeline after a short discovery call. Need to be live for a fixed campaign date? Ask about Expedited Launch above.
A variant is a new version of your page, such as a different headline, image or form. A test is how we judge it. On Silver and Gold, variants run as live A/B tests: visitors are split between versions at the same time until one wins with confidence. On Bronze, the new variant replaces the page and is compared month over month. Unused variants don’t roll over.
