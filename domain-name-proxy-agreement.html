Proxy Agreement

How our WHOIS privacy service works, what it protects, and what it doesn't.

Last updated: September 2026

// 01 — what this covers

A stand-in, not a handoff.

This agreement governs the domain privacy proxy service VardWeb provides on eligible domains registered through us. Instead of your personal contact information appearing in the public WHOIS database, we list a privacy proxy in its place. You keep full control of the domain, we just keep your name, address, phone number, and email out of a database anyone can query.

// 02 — you're still the registrant

You own it. We just answer the door.

The proxy service changes what's publicly visible, not who owns the domain. You remain the true, beneficial registrant of record on your account at all times. VardWeb, and any underlying registrar or registry we work with, never gains an ownership interest in your domain by providing this service.

// 03 — when the proxy can be revealed

Privacy isn't a shield for abuse.

We may reveal the underlying registrant information, or suspend the proxy, in response to a valid legal process (subpoena, court order), a credible law enforcement request, a UDRP or equivalent domain-dispute proceeding, a substantiated abuse complaint (phishing, malware, spam, IP infringement), or if the domain's registration lapses or the account falls delinquent on payment.

// 04 — eligibility restrictions

Not every ending allows this.

Some registries restrict or prohibit privacy protection for specific top-level domains as a condition of operating that extension. This is set by the registry, not by VardWeb, and applies no matter which registrar you use. If a domain you're registering isn't eligible, we'll tell you before you complete the purchase.

// 05 — how long it lasts

Tied to the domain, not the invoice.

Privacy protection applies for as long as the applicable domain is actively registered through VardWeb and remains eligible under its registry's rules. It ends if the domain expires, transfers away from VardWeb, or becomes ineligible under a registry policy change. It isn't a separate subscription you can lose independently of the domain itself.

// 06 — your responsibilities

Hidden from the public. Not from us.

Even though your details aren't publicly listed, you're still required to keep accurate registrant contact information on file with us; ICANN and registry rules require it, and inaccurate information can put the domain itself at risk regardless of the privacy proxy. If we or the registry need to reach you (renewal notices, abuse complaints, legal notices relayed through the proxy), you're responsible for responding.

// 07 — governing law

Canadian law. Same as everything else.

This agreement is governed by the laws of Canada and the province in which VardWeb operates, without regard to conflict-of-law principles, consistent with our Terms of Service.

// 08 — questions & contact

Questions about your privacy proxy?

If you have any questions about how domain privacy works, want to know whether a specific domain is eligible, or need to raise a concern about your listing, reach out directly. We respond promptly and without friction.
