Sending Questions, Answered

With pay-as-you-go pricing, you can continue sending beyond your monthly quota and only pay for what you use.

Overages are billed in buckets of 1,000 emails at your plan's overage rate. You can request to enable this feature at any time.

If you don't enable pay-as-you-go and exceed your plan limits, you will be notified and given the option to upgrade to a higher plan.

In VardWeb, transactional emails are emails sent using the /emails API, while marketing emails are sent using the /broadcasts API or through the Broadcasts interface in the dashboard.

Transactional emails are typically triggered by user actions and include things like password resets or order confirmations.

Marketing emails are typically sent to a list of contacts and include things like newsletters or promotional campaigns.

Broadcast emails can only be sent to existing contacts, while transactional emails can be sent to any email address.

Transactional and Marketing emails are billed separately.

For marketing plans, the focus is on maintaining a relationship with your contacts. Therefore marketing plans are not limited by the number of emails you can send, but only by the number of contacts you have.

Broadcasts can only be sent to existing contacts.

No. Broadcast emails sent through the /broadcasts API or through the Broadcasts interface in the dashboard do not count against your transactional email limits.

Only emails sent through the /emails API count towards your transactional email limits.

Each received email counts towards your transactional emails quota.
